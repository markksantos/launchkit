# FamShield — account-creation checklist

These are the accounts launchkit cannot create automatically (they require SMS / email / ID verification or new-account captcha). Each item is paste-ready: open the URL, paste the copy, verify, done. Tier 1 first.

## Tier 1 — entity / knowledge-graph

### LinkedIn Company Page

- **Sign-up URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/
- **Why:** Strongest entity-recognition signal for Google and AI knowledge graphs. Required for Organization schema sameAs.
- **Verification:** manual-review
- **Approval ETA:** a few hours after a personal account verifies the page
- **Notes:** Set the vanity URL to /getfamshield. Add #1B2A4A as accent color.

**Paste-ready copy:**

```text
FamShield — FamShield prepares estate planning documents for couples in about eleven minutes. The questionnaire walks both partners through a will, healthcare directive, power of attorney, optional guardianship, and HIPAA authorization, customizing each document with your selected state. Documents are ready for attorney review and signing. FamShield is document preparation, not legal advice.

Website: https://getfamshield.com
Vanity URL: linkedin.com/company/getfamshield
```

### Crunchbase organization

- **Sign-up URL:** https://www.crunchbase.com/add-new
- **Why:** Crunchbase is consumed by Wikidata and AI knowledge graphs. Real entity wiring.
- **Verification:** email
- **Approval ETA:** 1–7 days for moderation

**Paste-ready copy:**

```text
FamShield
Website: https://getfamshield.com
Founder: Mark Santos
Location: (your HQ)
Category: SaaS
Description: FamShield is a document preparation service built for couples who want to put their estate planning in place without spending three thousand dollars and six weeks. The questionnaire walks both partners through five documents — a will, advance healthcare directive, durable power of attorney, an optional guardianship designation for minor children, and a HIPAA authorization — in about eleven minutes. Each document is customized with the state you select, includes the witness and notary blocks that state expects, and ships as a printable PDF with structured signing instructions. Documents are template-based and intended for review by a licensed attorney before signing; FamShield is document preparation, not legal advice. Couples can generate a complete reciprocal set so each partner is the testator/principal/declarant of their own documents.
```
