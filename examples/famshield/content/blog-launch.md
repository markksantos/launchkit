Today I'm shipping **FamShield** — estate planning documents for couples in 11 minutes.

## What it does

FamShield is a document preparation service built for couples who want to put their estate planning in place without spending three thousand dollars and six weeks. The questionnaire walks both partners through five documents — a will, advance healthcare directive, durable power of attorney, an optional guardianship designation for minor children, and a HIPAA authorization — in about eleven minutes. Each document is customized with the state you select, includes the witness and notary blocks that state expects, and ships as a printable PDF with structured signing instructions. Documents are template-based and intended for review by a licensed attorney before signing; FamShield is document preparation, not legal advice. Couples can generate a complete reciprocal set so each partner is the testator/principal/declarant of their own documents.

## Who it's for

Couples in their 30s and 40s, especially new parents, who want a will and healthcare directive in place but balk at hiring a lawyer.

## Why this, why now

Two partners sit on the couch, fill out the questionnaire together, generate five documents customized to their state, and have them ready for attorney review before signing.

Existing options like Trust & Will, FreeWill, LegalZoom solve adjacent problems but not this one specifically.
